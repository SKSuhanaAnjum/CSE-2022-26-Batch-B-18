About AquaDetect

An integrated approach to assess river water quality using AI-powered detection

Project Overview

AquaDetect is an advanced system designed to automatically detect and classify various pollutants in river water using state-of-the-art object detection technology. Our system helps in monitoring water quality and identifying contamination sources efficiently.

By leveraging YOLOv10 (You Only Look Once version 10) object detection algorithm, we can identify multiple types of pollutants simultaneously with high accuracy, providing accurate insights into water conditions.

Key Features

🤖 AI Detection

Uses YOLOv10 for accurate identification of pollutants in water images

📊 Real-time Analysis

Instant processing and results with detailed pollution reports

🔔 Automated Alerts

Notifications to administrators when pollutants are detected

🌊 Multi-source Support

Applicable to rivers, lakes, and other water bodies

Pollutants Detected

Our system can detect the following types of pollutants and objects:

  • Chemical Pollutants: Acid contamination, chemical waste
  • Organic Waste: Dead animals, leaves, paper
  • Plastic Pollution: Bottles, bags, plastic containers
  • Other Objects: Glass, metal, cardboard, debris
  • Natural Indicators: Eutrophication (algal blooms), fish oil

Technology Stack

Built with modern technologies for reliable performance:

• Backend: Flask (Python), YOLOv10, OpenCV

• Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript

• Database: MySQL for user management

• Computer Vision: Custom-trained YOLOv10 model

Project Purpose

This project aims to address the growing concern of water pollution by providing an automated, efficient, and accurate system for water quality assessment. Traditional manual monitoring methods are time-consuming and often inadequate for comprehensive pollution detection.

AquaDetect bridges this gap by combining advanced AI technology with a user-friendly interface, making water quality monitoring accessible to researchers, environmental agencies, and concerned citizens alike.
